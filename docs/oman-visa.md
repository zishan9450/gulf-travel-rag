# Oman visas

Last reviewed January 2026. Entry rules change, so confirm them before you book a non-refundable fare.

Indian passport holders can apply for an Oman eVisa online before they fly. The form asks for a passport scan, a portrait photo, and the address where you will stay. Approval usually comes back as a PDF. Save it on your phone and print a copy, because the airline desk sometimes asks to see it before boarding.

## Who should not assume the same rule

A GCC residence visa is not the same thing as a passport. Someone living in Dubai on an Indian passport still applies with the Indian passport, though some resident categories get a shorter application. Holders of other passports may get a different stay length, or may need to apply through a sponsor. If your passport is not covered by the notes on this page, treat that as unknown rather than guessing from a neighbouring country.

## At the airport

Muscat International Airport (MCT) is the usual entry point. Have the passport that matches the eVisa, the approval PDF, and a ticket out of Oman or a clear onward plan. Immigration officers may ask where you are staying on the first night. Cash in Omani rials is useful after the desk, but cards work at the airport counters.

## Length of stay and overstay

Stay only for the number of days printed on the approval. Overstaying is a fine, and it can block the next visit. Extending a visit is a separate application; do not assume a hotel can do it on the morning you were supposed to leave. If your plans change, ask early on a weekday, not on a Friday afternoon.

## What this page does not cover

This note does not list fees, because they move. It also does not cover work permits, student residence, or driving a rental car across the border into the UAE. Those are different permissions. A tourist eVisa is for a visit, not for taking a job.
