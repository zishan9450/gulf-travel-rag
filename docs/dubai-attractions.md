# Dubai sights

Dubai is a city of separate districts, and the famous tower is only one of them. The observation deck people book is Burj Khalifa At The Top. Tickets are for a timed entry. Sunset sells out first. The mall wrapped around the tower is Dubai Mall, which is also the way most visitors reach the fountain show on the lake. The fountains run on a schedule through the afternoon and evening, not all night.

## Old Dubai

Al Fahidi and the Dubai Creek are the older scale of the city. The abra boats cross the creek for a small cash fare and connect the textile and gold souqs of Deira with the Al Fahidi side. Bargaining belongs here. The museum in Al Fahidi Fort is the compact version of how the town sat on the creek before the tower existed. Friday morning is a quiet time in the lanes.

## Beaches and the palm

Jumeirah's open beach is public. The hotel beaches on the Palm are for guests or for a day pass. A public beach still expects a cover-up when you walk to a restaurant. The Metro does not serve the Palm itself; a taxi or a tram connection is the realistic last step.

## How not to spend the day

Trying to see the creek, the tower, and a Palm hotel in one walking loop does not work. They are far apart. Pick one modern district and one older district, and leave the desert or the waterpark for another day. Summer midday is for indoors. The malls exist for that reason, not only for shopping.

## Tickets

Buy the tower ticket from the official deck, not from a stranger outside the mall. A "skip the line" offer that is not on the official site is the common scam in this building.
