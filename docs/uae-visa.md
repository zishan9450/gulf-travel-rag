# UAE visas

Last reviewed January 2026. The official source is the Federal Authority for Identity and Citizenship, and a hotel blog is not a substitute for it.

The UAE grants a visa on arrival for many passport holders, often for 30 or 90 days, depending on the passport. The stamp or the electronic record is the permission. You still need a passport with enough validity, usually six months, and some nationalities need a visa arranged before the flight instead of at the desk. If your passport is not on the published list, do not infer the rule from a friend's different passport.

## Residents and visit visas

A residence visa is for people who live and work in the country. A tourist or visit entry is not permission to take a job. Overstaying a visit is fined by the day. Extending, or doing a "visa run", is a policy that changes; ask the official channel rather than a taxi driver at Hatta.

## Airports

Dubai (DXB), Abu Dhabi (AUH), and Sharjah (SHJ) are the common entries. The desk you want is the one for visitors, not the smart-gate lane reserved for residents and citizens, unless the sign says your passport can use it. Have the address of your first hotel ready. Duty free is after baggage claim on the way out, not a place to solve a visa problem.

## What this page will not invent

It does not quote a fee, a processing hour, or a list of every passport. Those details are exactly the ones that go stale. Use it to understand the shape of the rule: some passports get a visa on arrival, others must arrange one first, and a residence visa is a different document.
